## Purpose and outcome

The initial container experiment compared CogletNet with common retrieval baselines while keeping its generation-model role aligned with other SUTs. Provider instability and native failures were attributed separately from answer quality.

## Interpretation and limits

Results are conditional on the pinned source, model services, embedding, input mode, coverage, and resume history; CogletNet is not the evaluation reference standard.
