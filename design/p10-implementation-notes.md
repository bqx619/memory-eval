## Purpose and outcome

Result cards expose provenance, coverage, metrics, costs, and comparison conditions. The index deduplicates resume generations and keeps unknown values null. Duplicate memory-only score leaves and per-episode billing rows are omitted from cards without altering raw reports.

## Interpretation and limits

Cards summarize evidence; they are not a leaderboard across incompatible protocols or incomplete coverage.
