# Historical designs and experiments

These records explain earlier questions, assumptions, corrections, and acceptance boundaries. They are not current runbooks. Original paths remain for source references. Long operational transcripts are condensed English publication records; the original document hashes distinguish them from complete transcripts. Private deployment and coordination details are excluded.

All SUT experiments are conditional on version, configuration, data, model, and coverage. Failures remain part of the interpretation; no SUT is a preferred scoring reference. See [current contracts](current-contracts.md).
